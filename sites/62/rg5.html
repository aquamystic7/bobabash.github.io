The Haunting

Some houses hold secrets. Others refuse to let them go.

When a young writer moves into an abandoned mansion, she expects to find inspiration for her next novel. Instead, she discovers strange whispers, mysterious shadows, and a locked room that appears to change every night.

As the truth slowly unfolds, she realizes that the house is not empty—and something inside knows her name.

Author: Eleanor Black
